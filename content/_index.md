Documents maintained by the [BBQS Working Group on Data Standards](https://brain-bbqs.org/working-groups/standards/):

- **Guidelines for BBQS Data Standards**: which data standards to use for BBQS behavior and neural data, and what to do when your data is not yet supported by them.
- **Working Group Charter**: goals, scope, and organization of the working group.
